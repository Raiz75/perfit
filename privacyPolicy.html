Privacy Policy for PERFIT

Last Updated: Sep 23, 2025

PERFIT values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when using our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you use PERFIT, we may collect the following on a temporary basis:

  • Personal Data: age, gender, and other basic profile information.
  • Assessment Data: your responses to skills, interest, passion, and behavioral profiling questions.
  • Usage Data: browser type, device information, and activity within the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Generate assessment results and recommendations for ministry fit.
  • Improve the accuracy and personalization of PERFIT.
  • Ensure the security and functionality of the platform.
  • Process assessment responses with the help of the OpenAI API for analysis and insights.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Data may only be shared with:
    • Authorized administrators of PERFIT (for support and improvements).
    • OpenAI API, strictly for processing and generating assessment results.
  • PERFIT does not permanently store your personal data. Information is processed during your session only.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • PERFIT uses session storage and in-memory arrays to temporarily hold your progress.
  • Your data is cleared when you close your browser or end your session.
  • No long-term database storage is maintained.

5. Your Rights

Since PERFIT does not permanently store personal data, there is no retained record to access, correct, or delete after your session ends.

  • During your active session, you may restart or clear your assessment at any time.

For questions or concerns, please contact us at raizeningalla@gmail.com.

6. Cookies and Tracking

PERFIT does not use cookies. Only session storage and arrays are used to keep track of your answers temporarily while you are taking the assessment.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last Updated” date.
